In a world filled with distractions and competing demands, setting the right priorities is essential for a strong Christian family. Jesus gave us a clear principle:

> "But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well." — Matthew 6:33

## The Right Order

The Bible establishes a clear order of priorities: God first, then family, then service to others, then work and everything else.

### 1. God First

Before anything else, we are called to love the Lord with all our heart, soul, mind, and strength. Our relationship with God must be the foundation of everything else.

### 2. Family Second

After God, our family is our most important responsibility. Quality time with family takes intentional effort. Here are some suggestions:

- **Regular family meals** — Put away distractions and talk to each other.
- **Family worship** — Set aside time to pray and read Scripture together.
- **One-on-one time** — Spend individual time with each child.
- **Date nights** — Protect time with your spouse.

### 3. Work and Ministry Third

Work is important, but it is not our identity. When God and family come first, our work often becomes more productive because we have peace and order in our lives.

## Guarding Your Time

Time is one of the most precious gifts we have. Be careful what you allow to consume your family's time and attention. Screen time especially needs to be held in check.

> "Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, making the best use of the time." — Ephesians 5:15-16

## Conclusion

When we place God first and family second, everything else finds its proper place. A family aligned with God's priorities will experience His blessing and peace.
