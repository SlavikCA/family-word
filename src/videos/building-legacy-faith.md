In this conference session, we discuss the importance of building a spiritual legacy that will outlive us. Our children and grandchildren should inherit more than material possessions — they should inherit a living faith.

## Session Topics

- Passing on your testimony to the next generation.
- Creating family traditions that center on Christ.
- Mentoring young people in the faith.
- The responsibility of the older generation to disciple the younger.

> "One generation commends your works to another; they tell of your mighty acts." — Psalm 145:4

Let us labor now so that a strong legacy of faith will be left for generations to come.
