Marriage is one of the most beautiful gifts God has given to humanity. From the very beginning, in the Garden of Eden, God established marriage as a covenant relationship between one man and one woman.

## Christ at the Center

A godly marriage is built on a relationship with Jesus Christ. When both spouses seek to follow Christ first, their marriage naturally grows stronger. The Apostle Paul writes:

> "Wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands as you do to the Lord. For the husband is the head of the wife as Christ is the head of the church. Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her." — Ephesians 5:22-25

## Practical Steps for a Strong Marriage

1. **Pray together daily** — Prayer unites your hearts and invites God into your relationship.
2. **Study the Word together** — A shared time in Scripture builds spiritual intimacy.
3. **Practice forgiveness** — No marriage is perfect; forgiveness is essential for healing.
4. **Serve one another** — Look for ways to bless your spouse each day.

## A Lifelong Journey

Marriage is a journey, not a destination. There will be seasons of joy and seasons of challenge, but with Christ at the center, every season can be a season of growth. Commit today to building your marriage on the Rock — Jesus Christ.
