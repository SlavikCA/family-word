In this sermon, we explore the vital role of prayer in building a strong Christian family. When we pray together, we invite God into our homes and allow Him to work in our hearts and relationships.

## Key Points

- Prayer is not just a ritual — it is a relationship with God.
- A praying family is a united family.
- Praying with your children teaches them that God is real and personal.
- God honors the prayer of a family that seeks Him together.

> "For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them." — Matthew 18:20

May this sermon encourage you to make prayer a central part of your family life.
