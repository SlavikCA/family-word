Raising children is one of the greatest responsibilities God has given to parents. It is also one of the greatest privileges. The Bible tells us to train our children in the ways of the Lord:

> "Start children off on the way they should go, and even when they are old they will not turn from it." — Proverbs 22:6

## The Home as the First Church

The home is the first place where children learn about God. Parents are their children's first pastors and teachers. What happens at home often shapes a child's faith more than anything else.

### Daily Habits that Build Faith

- **Family devotions** — Set aside a regular time each day to read Scripture and pray together.
- **Modeled faith** — Children learn more from what you do than what you say. Let them see you pray, worship, and trust God.
- **Open conversation** — Create a safe place where children can ask questions about faith without fear.
- **Service to others** — Involve your children in acts of kindness and generosity.

## Training with Love

Discipline must always be rooted in love. God disciplines us because He loves us, and we should do the same for our children. Avoid harshness; instead, be consistent, patient, and full of grace.

## The Greatest Legacy

There is no greater legacy you can leave your children than a living faith in Jesus Christ. Pray for them faithfully, teach them diligently, and love them unconditionally.
